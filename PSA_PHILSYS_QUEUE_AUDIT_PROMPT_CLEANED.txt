PHASE 7 — TRANSFER BEHAVIOR
==================================================

### 9. Fix Transferred Queue Workflow

When a queue is transferred:

- Set or retain a clear TRANSFERRED status/label where appropriate.
- Do not immediately mark the queue as CATERED/SERVING merely because it was transferred.
- The transferred queue must be added to the receiving staff member/window's queue/list.
- The receiving staff member must explicitly cater/call it.
- Do not automatically start the catering timer before the receiving staff member actually caters it.
- Preserve the original service/window information in history.
- Record the transfer destination.
- Record transfer time.
- Ensure public display and realtime/refresh state are accurate.

Expected lifecycle:

Window A -> Transfer -> Transferred -> appears in Window B / receiving queue -> Window B explicitly caters/calls -> Serving/Catered -> timer starts.

Invalid lifecycle:

Transfer -> automatically catered/serving without receiving staff action.
